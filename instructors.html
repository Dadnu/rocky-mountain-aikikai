Instructors

Long-time students who now teach the art they've practiced for decades

Dave Marlow

Dave started martial arts practicing Shotokan karate and Muay Thai. He became a student of Aikido with Rocky Mountain Aikikai in 1994, he holds the rank of Godan (5th degree black belt). He retired from the Colorado State Patrol and is currently a commercial pilot. He and his wife Debra are the proud parents of three children. Hobbies include mules, ranching, honey bees, camping, hunting, fishing, flying, and the guitar.

Geoff Goodman

Geoff began his practice of martial arts while stationed overseas with the U.S. Air Force. He practiced Shotokan karate and Hakkoryu jujitsu in Japan and taekwondo while in Vietnam. He has been practicing Aikido with Rocky Mountain Aikikai since 1985, where he has achieved the rank of Sandan (3rd degree black belt). He also practices and teaches the Chinese art of Qigong, a system of physical exercises for wellness and healing. Geoff is retired from the U.S. Air Force and from the U.S. Department of Homeland Security. He is married and has a son, a daughter and two grandsons.
