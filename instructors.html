Instructors

Long-time students who now teach the art they've practiced for decades

Dave Marlow

Godan (5th degree black belt) [confirm rank]

Dave began his martial arts training in Shotokan karate and Muay Thai. He became a student of aikido with Rocky Mountain Aikikai in 1994 and is the club's president and main contact.

He retired from the Colorado State Patrol and now flies as a commercial pilot. He and his wife Debra have three children. Away from the mat you'll find him with his mules, honey bees and guitar, or ranching, camping, hunting, fishing and flying.

Geoff Goodman

Sandan (3rd degree black belt) [confirm rank]

Geoff began practicing martial arts while stationed overseas with the U.S. Air Force, studying Shotokan karate and Hakkoryu jujitsu in Japan and taekwondo in Vietnam. He has practiced aikido with Rocky Mountain Aikikai since 1985.

He also practices and teaches qigong, a Chinese system of exercises for wellness and healing. Geoff is retired from the U.S. Air Force and the U.S. Department of Homeland Security. He is married, with a son, a daughter and two grandsons.
